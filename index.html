KVYO, also known as KAYO Collectives, is a clothing brand that was first imagined in 2024 and officially brought to life in 2026. What started as a simple idea eventually grew into a vision of creating a brand that speaks to everyone. That's where the name "KAYO" came from. In Filipino, kayo means you, representing the people who inspire and wear the brand.

Founded by Charles James, a working student with a passion for fashion and creativity, KAYO Collectives was built with the goal of offering something different. Rather than focusing solely on streetwear, the brand embraces a variety of clothing styles, giving people more ways to express their personality and individuality through fashion.
